# MD Operating Dashboard: assumptions and evidence register

Reviewed: 6 September 2026. This is a proposed planning model, not reported company results, a valuation, or an approved budget.

## MD decision principles

Start with firm scope and contractual obligations. Separate contract value, acceptance-based revenue, cash receipts and delivery costs. Treat strategic partnerships as multi-year execution systems; recognise revenue only from defined commercial rights. Hire against funded work and specialist capacity. Market budgets and platform fit do not establish Shield sales.

## Numerical planning inputs

Defaults are starting inputs for sensitivity testing. They are not estimates validated against company contracts or payroll. The base case starts at an assumed $20M; all financial outputs calculate immediately.

### Assumed Shield contract value

Default: 20 (USD millions), assumed over three years.

$20M assumed package = $12M hardware, ground equipment and initial spares + $5M Hivemind licences / SDK + $3M integration, training and initial support. Each allowance is a planning assumption, not a quote or disclosed Army price. It defines example scope, not an accounting allocation. Optional follow-ons and JSW investment are excluded. The total is editable in the dashboard.

Sensitivity: Test $12M / $20M / $30M as smaller / base / expanded scope cases, not market-price confidence bounds. Each $1M changes lifetime revenue by $1M and modelled external delivery cost by $0.65M at the default rate.

Validation owner: Finance + Commercial

Evidence required: Executed contract, firm purchase orders, performance obligations and invoicing entity.

### Planning team

Default: 31 (people, not actual headcount).

31 is carried from the previous illustrative deck only; no verified roster supports it. Model holds this team flat across four years, with no automatic hiring.

Sensitivity: One additional person adds $60k/year at the default loaded cost. Test 20 / 31 / 40 posts; these are planning cases, not staffing estimates.

Validation owner: People + function leads

Evidence required: India employee roster, start dates, contractors and direct/indirect allocation.

### Loaded people cost

Default: 60000 (USD / person / year).

$60k is a provisional budgeting input, not a salary benchmark. Includes pay, benefits and employment costs; excludes global engineering support and office overhead.

Sensitivity: $45k–$90k per person is an unvalidated stress range. A $10k change adds $310k/year for 31 posts.

Validation owner: People + Finance

Evidence required: Payroll by role, benefits, employment costs, contractors and approved FX rate.

### Local non-people overhead

Default: 0.6 (USD millions / year).

$0.6M provision for premises, tools, travel and local professional services. Excludes JSW plant capex, HQ R&D, financing and taxes.

Sensitivity: $0.4M / $0.6M / $1M are planning stress cases. Every $0.1M changes annual cash need by $0.1M.

Validation owner: Finance + Operations

Evidence required: Office leases, tool licences, travel plan and local service contracts; eliminate payroll overlap.

### External programme cost

Default: 65 (% of firm Shield contract value).

65% is an unvalidated sensitivity input for bought-in hardware, global product charges, partner work, logistics and warranty provision. Local payroll is modelled separately to prevent double counting.

Sensitivity: Test 55% / 65% / 75%; each 10-point change costs $1M on a $10M contract. No product-specific margin is asserted.

Validation owner: Procurement + Global Product + Finance

Evidence required: Costed BOM, global transfer prices, partner statement of work, freight, warranty and cost-to-complete.

### Customer advance

Default: 20 (% at model month 1).

20% is a cash-planning case, not a reported Army payment term. Advance is cash, not revenue. Retention remains 10%; progress receipts share is 90% minus this input.

Sensitivity: Stress zero advance before committing supplier cash. Bank-guarantee collateral and fees must be added separately.

Validation owner: Commercial + Treasury

Evidence required: Payment clause, bank guarantees, advance recovery and enforceability.

### Collection after acceptance

Default: 3 (months).

Three months is a stress-planning convention, not an observed collection cycle. Applies to progress payments after each acceptance gate.

Sensitivity: Test 1 / 3 / 6 months. A delay changes cash timing, not assumed accepted revenue.

Validation owner: Finance + Programmes

Evidence required: Invoice prerequisites, acceptance certification and customer payment history.

## Model rules

### Model boundary & currency

India-facing Shield programme economics, not the statutory P&L of Shield AI India. USD throughout; no exchange-rate claim. Actual sales, backlog, cash and EBITDA remain unknown.

Owner: Finance

Validate: Confirm legal entities, invoicing, intercompany charging, opening backlog and bank balances before treating outputs as a budget.

### Multi-year delivery

A three-year execution case with acceptance in months 9 / 21 / 33, allocating 20% / 50% / 30% of entered contract value. Month 1 is mobilisation, not today or the announcement date. This is a proposed sequence, not a reported Army schedule.

Owner: Programmes + customer acceptance owner

Validate: Replace with contractual delivery lots, acceptance criteria, licence obligations and support periods. Planning acceptance allocation is not an accounting recognition conclusion.

### Delay sensitivity

The delayed case shifts every acceptance and related supplier outflow six months. It assumes procurement can be rescheduled; non-deferrable supplier commitments could produce a larger cash gap. Team costs continue throughout the four-year view.

Owner: Programmes + Procurement

Validate: Contracted supplier due dates, inventory exposure, customer extensions and recovery staffing.

### Cash & cost timing

External supplier cash paid three months before each acceptance; the contribution view matches these costs to accepted scope. Advance at month 1; remaining progress cash after acceptance plus collection lag; 10% retention released 12 months after final acceptance. Opening dedicated cash is zero. Financing, tax, guarantees and exceptional capex are excluded.

Owner: Treasury + Finance

Validate: Treasury cash baseline and month-by-month supplier / customer cash schedule. Receipts beyond month 48 are shown separately, not silently counted as collected.

### Product / Software / Services

The Army announcement supports product and software scope. Services such as training and integration require a priced statement of work. The base-package allowances are planning inputs, not actual segment sales or margins.

Owner: Commercial + Product

Validate: Use mutually exclusive priced contract lines. Until then the dashboard filters programme scope without reporting the assumed split as actual segment revenue or margin.

### JSW strategic partnership

Track technology transfer, industrial readiness and future order releases. The announced $90M belongs to JSW’s investment programme. Royalties, licence fees, minimum purchases and export allocations are unpriced and excluded.

Owner: MD + JSW counterpart + Legal

Validate: Licence agreement, fee schedule, minimum order commitments, production acceptance and export rights. Investment announcement alone creates no Shield revenue entitlement.

### New customer & OEM routes

Navy, maritime partners and Indian platform OEMs remain strategic pursuits from the opportunity map. No signed orders, proposal stage, close date or dollar pipeline is inferred from market size or technical fit.

Owner: BD + Solutions Engineering

Validate: Named sponsor, funded requirement, partner access, trial results and a firm commercial instrument. Optional support renewals are excluded until contracted.

## Computation and review

Annual people cost = planning posts × loaded annual cost. Annual local run cost = people cost + non-people overhead. External programme cost is modelled separately from local payroll. Contract revenue follows acceptance shares of 20%, 50%, 30%; customer cash comprises the entered advance, progress receipts totalling 90% less the advance, and 10% retention. Closing cash is cumulative receipts less supplier outflows and local run cost, with zero opening dedicated cash. Peak funding is the largest negative monthly cumulative cash balance, not annual operating loss.

Actual company performance remains unpopulated. Replace defaults with a signed contract schedule, costed BOM and partner scope, employee roster, intercompany allocation and treasury cash plan before making commitments. The model does not establish India-entity accounting revenue; Finance must validate recognition for each performance obligation.

## Weekly / monthly update discipline

Weekly: validate customer acceptance gates, supplier timing and partner responsibilities; record changes to forecasts against agreed dates. Monthly: reconcile actual receipts, payroll and cost-to-complete, and refresh funding needs. The present implementation is session-only; it does not save an approved forecast or ingest operational actuals.
